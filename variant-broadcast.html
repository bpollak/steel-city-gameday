Step One

Get your crew
on the field.

A crew is your private circle — the family thread, the college group, the six people who text through every snap. One tap sends an invite; nobody outside gets in.

  • 1

    Start a crew

    Name it, invite your people, done. Run as many as you want — the Pollak Family and the College Buddies can live side by side.

  • 2

    One room per game

    Every matchup spins up its own room with a live kickoff countdown, so the trash talk always has a home.

  • 3

    Private by default

    No public timeline, no strangers, no algorithm. Just your crew and the game.

The Broadcast

Three acts of a
Steel City Sunday.

Pregame, kickoff, final. Every crew moves through the same broadcast — and the app keeps the whole box score.

01 PregameBefore kickoff

Call your shots.

Before the anthem, everyone locks in predictions worth real points. Multiple choice on the big moments, closest-guess-wins on the numbers — total points, passing yards, the works. Change your call right up until kickoff, then it's sealed.
