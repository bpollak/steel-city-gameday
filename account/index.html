About Signing In

Why an account is required to use Steel City Gameday

What your account connects

  • Your private Crew: membership, invitations, role, display name, and the people you play with.
  • Your game-day activity: picks, live votes, points, standings, receipts, messages, and reactions.
  • Your Season Pass: the entitlement linked to your Steel City Gameday account after Apple confirms the purchase, so access can be restored across devices.
  • Your safety controls: reports, blocks, and server-side access rules that keep private Crew content available only to signed-in members.

Why this requires sign-in

Steel City Gameday is built around private groups and a season-long competition. The service must know which Crew you belong to, which activity is yours, what you can access, and where your season progress should be saved. An account provides that continuity when you change devices, reinstall the app, or return for the next game.

Your sign-in choices

Use Sign in with Apple or create an account with an email address and password. Sign in with Apple gives us an app-specific account identifier; it does not give us your Apple ID password. For email accounts, our authentication provider securely manages the password credential, and we cannot view your password.

Privacy and control

We use account information to provide, secure, and support the service. We do not sell personal information, serve ads, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Crews are private and invite-only, with no public feed.

You can permanently delete your account and account-linked content in the app at Settings → Delete account. Read the Privacy Policy for complete details.

Steel City Gameday is an independent, unofficial fan experience. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Pittsburgh Steelers or the NFL.
