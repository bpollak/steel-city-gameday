What's new, week by week.

Updates

Newest first · Update from the App Store

1.0.7 · September 21, 2026

Flash Picks you won't miss, and receipts that come to you.

  • Flash Picks arrive as a push notification, give you five minutes to answer, and show on the Lock Screen with a countdown that leaves when the pick locks.
  • When the game goes final you get a notification, and the Room gets a "Receipts are in" card that jumps to that week's receipts.
  • Every resolved question on Receipts shows the answer and who took the points.
  • The Room only posts a score line when the score changes or a quarter ends.
  • Fixed a sent message reappearing in the composer.
  • The message check now catches only a short list of slurs and self-harm phrases, matched as whole words. Report and block are unchanged.

1.0.6 · September 20, 2026

Game Day always comes back to this week's game.

  • Opening the app from an older Crew notification could leave the Room on a finished game. A notice now marks that room and a "This week" button takes you back.
  • Stale live flags on the schedule no longer pull Game Day to the wrong game.

1.0.5 · September 16, 2026

Replies with context, and fairer Crew scoring.

  • Replies now show what they're replying to. Tap the quote to jump back to it.
  • Fixed a message posting twice after dictating it.
  • Emoji-only messages send instead of being flagged.
  • A Crew only scores the games it was around for: join after kickoff and that game doesn't count in that Crew.
  • Receipts and pick counts show only your Crew's picks.

1.0.3 · September 14, 2026

Game day, live in the app.

  • The Room header shows kickoff, the live score with the quarter and clock, and the final, updating on its own.
  • A Lock Screen and Dynamic Island card follows the game with the score and the open Flash Pick. Requires iOS 17.2 or later and one launch of the app after installing.
  • Your Crew chat continues from week to week, with a divider at each game.
  • Flash Picks fire on predictable moments through the game.
  • Steelers stickers in the chat: tap the football icon in the composer.
  • The Season tab shows the Steelers record and every final score.
  • Receipts and standings tally automatically after the final whistle.

1.0.2 · September 12, 2026

Crew notifications.

  • Optional push notifications when your Crew sends a message. See who sent it and reply right from the alert.
  • Set each Crew to All, Mentions and replies, or Muted in Settings, or mute a Crew for the current game.
  • The Room remembers where you were reading and keeps your message draft when you step away.
  • @mentions stand out in chat. Picks, Room, and Receipts share a compact score header, with better support for larger text.

1.0.1 · September 10, 2026

Crew chat reliability.

  • Messages appear more reliably for you and other Crew members.
  • Chat recovers more smoothly after a connection interruption or expired sign-in.
  • Names and recent message history display more consistently.
  • Improved message drafting, duplicate-send prevention, and keyboard dismissal.

1.0 · September 10, 2026

First release on the App Store.

  • Private, invite-only Crews for the people you watch Steelers games with.
  • Pregame picks that lock at kickoff, and timed Flash Picks during the game.
  • A Crew Room for each game with messages, reactions, and the current score.
  • Game receipts and season standings.
  • The Wire: Steelers headlines from original publishers.
  • Crew chat and your first five played games free; a one-time 2026 Season Pass for the rest of the season.
