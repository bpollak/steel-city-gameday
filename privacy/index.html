Privacy Policy

Steel City Gameday · Effective August 29, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple we receive a unique identifier and, if you share it, your name. We use it only to run your account.
  • Your content: Crews you create, and the messages, reactions, and predictions you post. Crew content is private to Crew members.
  • Device token: if you enable notifications, a push token so we can deliver them. It is not linked to an advertising identity.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, no ad tracking, and no selling or sharing your data with data brokers.
  • No contact-book upload. Invites go through your own share sheet.
  • Crew content is never used for anything outside your Crew.

Where it lives

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers, Supabase and Vercel, in the United States. Row-level access controls restrict each Crew’s content to its members.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, Crews you own, messages, and picks. You can also email us to request deletion.
