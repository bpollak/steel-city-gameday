Scored Crowned Remembered
The Sunday, start to finish

Four moves. One thread.

Everything a game day already is in your group chat — just with a scoreboard bolted on and a memory that lasts past Tuesday.

01

Get your Crew together

Start a private Crew, drop the invite link into the group text, and pull your people in — the family thread, the college group, the friends scattered across four time zones. Invite-only, no strangers, no feed.

  • One tap to spin up a Crew and share the link
  • Every Crew gets its own room, every game
  • A next-game countdown so nobody misses kickoff

02

Call your shots

Before kickoff, everyone locks in predictions — who wins, how close it is, total points, passing yards. Multiple choice for the big calls, real numbers for the ones worth arguing about. Nobody sees anyone's picks until they lock.

  • Closest guess in your Crew takes the points
  • Picks stay hidden until they lock at kickoff
  • Fresh questions written every single week

03

Watch it together

One private room per game. Messages, one-tap reactions — and Flash Picks that drop mid-drive with a ticking clock. Two minutes left, Steelers ball: does this end in a touchdown, a field goal, a punt, or a pick? Answer against the clock.

  • Live predictions that land while the drive is still alive
  • Results hit the chat the moment they're official
  • Works on the couch or three states away

04

Keep the receipts

Your Crew's picks are the receipts — proof of who actually called it. After the whistle every call is revealed side by side, the right ones checked off, the game's winner crowned, and the cards land in the chat your Crew will be quoting all week.

  • A scoreboard for the game, and one for the season
  • Share cards built to end the argument
  • Past games stay open — a record, not a feed
