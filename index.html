Steel City Gameday How it works

Your group chat, built for game day.

Private group chats for the people you watch Steelers games with, one Crew for family, another for college friends, with a fantasy football twist. Each Crew answers poll questions before and during the game, earns points for correct answers, and keeps a running tally all season.

During the game · Crew Room

Chat with your Crew during the game.

Each game has a Crew Room. Members send messages and reactions, see the current score and game clock, and answer timed Flash Picks while the game is in progress.

Invite-only Crews

A Crew is a private group for friends and family. People can join only with its invite link. There is no public feed.

Timed Flash Picks

Each live poll shows its point value and the time remaining to answer. Answers stay hidden from other members until the poll closes.

Before kickoff · Pregame Picks

Make predictions before kickoff.

Answer multiple-choice and numeric questions about the upcoming game, such as who wins, total points, and first touchdown. Each question shows how many points a correct or closest answer is worth.

Edit picks until kickoff

You can change an answer until the pick locks. Your most recent saved answer is the one that counts.

Picks stay private until they lock

Other Crew members cannot see your answers while a pick is open.

After the game · Receipts and Standings

Review results and season standings.

After the game, receipts show each question, every member's answer, the correct result, and points earned. Points from each game are added to a running regular-season total.

Game receipts

Correct choices earn the listed points, and numeric questions award points to the closest answer. The member with the most points is shown as the game winner.

Season standings

Standings cover the regular season, with checkpoints at Week 4, Week 9, Week 14, and Week 18. Playoffs are not included.

Also in the app · The Wire

Steelers news from original publishers.

The Wire checks publisher feeds every 15 minutes, lists the newest stories first, and groups articles that cover the same topic. Tap a headline to read the full story on the publisher's website.

The basics

Common questions.

What is Steel City Gameday?

A private iPhone app for Steelers fans who want to share game day, even when they aren't watching from the same place. It brings Crew chat, pregame picks, live polls, and season-long standings into one app.

Are Crews private?

Yes. There's no public feed. Only people with your Crew invite link can join, and nobody sees your picks until they lock.

Is Steel City Gameday free?

Crew chat and your first five played games are free. After that, a one-time 2026 Season Pass covers scored picks, receipts, and season standings. It does not renew.

Is this a betting app?

No. Picks are for entertainment inside your Crew. There is no wagering, entry fee, or prize of monetary value.

When and where can I get it?
