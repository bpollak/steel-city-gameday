Private groups · Crews

Create or join a private Crew.

A Crew is an invite-only group for friends and family. Crew members can chat, make picks, compare results, and compete in the same season standings.

Chat before and during the game

Each game has a Crew Room where members can send messages and reactions.

Invite-only access

People can join your Crew only when they have its invite link.

Before kickoff · Pregame Picks

Make predictions before kickoff.

Answer multiple-choice and numeric questions about the upcoming game. Each question shows how many points a correct or closest answer is worth.

Edit picks until kickoff

You can change an answer until the pick locks. Your most recent saved answer is the one that counts.

Picks stay private until they lock

Other Crew members cannot see your answers while a pick is open.

During the game · Crew Room

Chat and answer live polls during the game.

The Crew Room shows your group chat, the current score and game clock, and timed Flash Picks that appear while the game is in progress.

Answer timed Flash Picks

Each live poll shows its point value and the time remaining to answer.

Keep live picks private

Answers remain hidden from other Crew members until the poll closes.

After the game · Receipts

Review every pick and score after the game.

Receipts show each question, every member's answer, the correct result, points earned, and the game's highest scorer.

See how points were awarded

Correct choices earn the listed points. Numeric questions award points to the closest answer.

See the game winner

The member with the most points is shown as the winner. Tied members share the top result.

Across the season · Standings

Track total points across the regular season.

Points from each game are added to every member's regular-season total. The standings show rankings, progress checkpoints, and upcoming games.

View season checkpoints

The app marks Week 4 as Q1, Week 9 as halftime, Week 14 as Q3, and Week 18 as the regular-season final.

See upcoming games

The schedule shows the Crew's next matchup. The competition covers the regular season and does not include playoffs.

News · The Wire

Read Steelers news from original publishers.

The Wire checks publisher feeds every 15 minutes, lists the newest Steelers stories first, and groups articles that cover the same topic.

See recent Steelers news

Browse stories about injuries, roster changes, practices, matchups, and other team updates.

Open the original article

Tap any headline to read the complete story on the publisher's website.

The basics

Common questions.

What is Steel City Gameday?

Steel City Gameday is a private iPhone app for Steelers fans who want to share game day, even when they aren't watching from the same place. It brings Crew chat, pregame picks, live polls, and season-long standings into one app.

Are Crews private?

Yes. There's no public feed. Only people with your Crew invite link can join.

How often does The Wire update?

The Wire checks publisher feeds every 15 minutes. You can also pull to refresh, and every headline links to the original story.

Can I change a Pregame Pick?

Yes. You can change a Pregame Pick until it locks at kickoff. The last answer you save is the one that counts.

Can my Crew see what I picked?

Not while a pick is open. Choices stay private until the pick locks. Your Crew can still talk about their calls in the chat.

How do picks and scoring work?

Pregame Picks and Flash Picks each show their point value. Correct choices score, and numeric questions go to the closest guess. After the game, receipts reveal the calls and crown the top scorer.

Does the season total reset?

No. Every point stays in one running total through the regular season. The checkpoints are Week 4 for Q1, Week 9 for halftime, Week 14 for Q3, and Week 18 for the final. Playoffs are not included.

Is Steel City Gameday free?

Crew chat and your first five played games are free. After that, a one-time 2026 Season Pass covers scored picks, receipts, and season standings. It does not renew.

Why is an account required?

Your account keeps your private Crew membership, picks, points, messages, safety controls, and Season Pass connected to you across devices. Sign in with Apple or use an email address. More about signing in.

Is this a betting app?

No. Picks are for entertainment inside your Crew. There is no wagering, entry fee, or prize of monetary value.

Where will Steel City Gameday be available?

Steel City Gameday will be on iPhone for the 2026 season.
