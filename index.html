Get your Crew together for Week 2 at New England. Update to 1.0.3 and the score, the clock, and the open Flash Pick follow the game on their own, in the Room and on your Lock Screen. See every update.

Live score in the Room

The header shows kickoff, then the live score with the quarter and clock, then the final, updating on its own. Flash Picks fire on predictable moments through the game.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island card

A game card follows the score and the open Flash Pick without opening the app. Requires iOS 17.2 or later and one launch of the app after installing.

One chat all season

Your Crew chat continues from week to week, with a divider at each game. Tap the football icon in the composer for Steelers stickers.

Record, finals, and receipts

The Season tab shows the Steelers record and every final score. Receipts and standings tally automatically after the final whistle.

During the game · Crew Room

Chat with your Crew during the game.

Each game has a Crew Room. Members send messages and reactions, see the current score and game clock, and answer timed Flash Picks while the game is in progress.

Invite-only Crews

A Crew is a private group for friends and family. People can join only with its invite link. There is no public feed.

Timed Flash Picks

Each live poll shows its point value and the time remaining to answer. Answers stay hidden from other members until the poll closes.

Before kickoff · Pregame Picks

Make predictions before kickoff.

Answer multiple-choice and numeric questions about the upcoming game, such as who wins, total points, and first touchdown. Each question shows how many points a correct or closest answer is worth.

Edit picks until kickoff

You can change an answer until the pick locks. Your most recent saved answer is the one that counts.

Picks stay private until they lock

Other Crew members cannot see your answers while a pick is open.

After the game · Receipts and Standings

Review results and season standings.

After the game, receipts show each question, every member's answer, the correct result, and points earned. Points from each game are added to a running regular-season total.

Game receipts

Correct choices earn the listed points, and numeric questions award points to the closest answer. The member with the most points is shown as the game winner.

Season standings

Standings cover the regular season, with checkpoints at Week 4, Week 9, Week 14, and Week 18. Playoffs are not included. The Season tab also shows the Steelers record and every final score.

Also in the app · The Wire

Steelers news from original publishers.

The Wire checks publisher feeds every 15 minutes, lists the newest stories first, and groups articles that cover the same topic. Tap a headline to read the full story on the publisher's website.

The basics

Common questions.

What is Steel City Gameday?

A private Pittsburgh Steelers fan app for iPhone. Built for fans who want to share game day, even when they aren't watching from the same place. It brings Crew chat, pregame picks, live Flash Picks, Steelers news, and season-long standings into one mobile app.

Are Crews private?

Yes. There's no public feed. Only people with your Crew invite link can join, and nobody sees your picks until they lock.

Can I control Crew notifications?

Yes. Notifications are optional. In Settings > Notifications, choose All, Mentions and replies, or Muted for each Crew. You can also mute a Crew for the current game. Requires version 1.0.2 or later.

How much does Steel City Gameday cost?

The app is free to download, and Crew chat is always free. Scored picks, receipts, and season standings are free for your first five played games. After that they require the 2026 Season Pass, a one-time $9.99 in-app purchase for the 2026 season. It does not renew, and there are no other purchases in the app.

Is this a betting app?

No. Picks are for entertainment inside your Crew. There is no wagering, entry fee, or prize of monetary value.

When and where can I get it?
